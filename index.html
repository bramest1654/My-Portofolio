A digital companion that flags gaming addiction risk and behavior patterns. I built the Flutter front end for a four member team, with a cyber-glassmorphism UI, an interactive radar chart, and self evaluation sliders, integrating real-time risk scores from teammates' Random Forest and K-Means models through a FastAPI backend.
